Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 July 2026

MatchPoint collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. The app makes no network requests at all.

What the app stores

Everything MatchPoint saves stays in the app's own storage on your iPhone:

  • the player or team names you type
  • the match in progress, so it survives closing the app
  • your finished matches, with their scores and the time of each point
  • your settings — scoring rules, voice announcements, screen brightness

None of this is transmitted anywhere. It is not backed up to us, because there is no "us" to back it up to — MatchPoint has no server, no database and no account system. Nobody but you can read it.

What the app does not do

  • No tracking. No analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, no third-party trackers of any kind.
  • No account. You never sign in, and you never give the app an email address or a name it could tie to you.
  • No location. The app never asks for it and cannot use it.
  • No camera or microphone. The app has no permission for either. Spoken scores are produced by iOS itself, on the device.
  • No network. MatchPoint works identically in aeroplane mode, because it never connects to anything.

Deleting your data

Clear the history from inside the app — Match History, then the bin icon, for one match or for all of them. Deleting the app removes everything else with it. There is no copy anywhere else to ask us to delete.

Children

MatchPoint is safe for any age. Since it collects nothing at all, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If a future version ever does collect something — the planned rally-recording feature would need the camera — this page will say so before that version ships, and iOS will ask your permission separately.
