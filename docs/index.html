MatchPoint

A scorekeeper for tennis and padel. Put the phone on the bench, tap your side when you win a point, and it calls the score out loud.

Getting started

  1. New Match. Type the names — or leave them blank and play as Side 1 and Side 2. Choose singles or doubles, and who serves first.
  2. Turn the phone sideways. The score screen is landscape so it reads from across the court.
  3. Tap your half of the screen when your side wins a point. The score is spoken after each one.

Questions

The score is announced but I want quiet

Tap the bell in the sidebar of the score screen to mute, or turn off Voice Announcements in Settings.

I tapped the wrong side

Undo is the arrow in the sidebar. It takes back the point and cancels the announcement that was about to play. You can undo as many points as you like.

What happens at 40:40?

That depends on the rule you choose in Settings, and it is a real difference between competitions:

  • Like in tennis — win two points in a row. Advantages never run out.
  • Star point — up to two advantages; if the score comes back to 40:40 a third time, the next point wins the game. This is the rule the 2026 professional padel tour plays.
  • Golden point — the very next point wins the game. The usual choice in clubs.

Star point and golden point are padel rules. Tennis matches always play advantages, so the setting is hidden for tennis.

Does it handle tie-breaks?

Yes. At six games all the app switches to a tie-break to seven, counting in plain numbers rather than 15/30/40, alternating the serve one point then two, and giving the next set to the pair that did not open the tie-break — as the rulebook requires.

Why does my screen stay on?

So a match is not interrupted by unlocking the phone between points. Brightness is also raised while a match is running, which you can switch off in Settings. Both stop as soon as the match ends.

Can I delete one match without clearing everything?

Yes. Tap a match in Match History to open it, then the bin in the corner. The bin in the header of the list clears the whole history.

Does the app work without internet?

Entirely. MatchPoint never connects to anything — see the privacy policy.

Which rules does it follow?

The FIP Rules of Padel, 1 January 2026 edition, and standard tennis scoring.
